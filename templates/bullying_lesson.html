🛡️ Cyberbullying

Be Kind, Get Help!

Learn how to spot cyberbullying and what to do if it happens to you or someone else.

🚫

Don't Fight Back

It's tempting to send a mean reply back, but that usually just makes things worse. The best move is to block the person, not battle them.

💡 Remember
Block first, don't reply. Fighting back rarely helps.
🗣️

Always Tell a Trusted Adult

You never have to deal with a bully by yourself. Telling a parent, teacher, or trusted adult isn't tattling — it's asking for backup.

💡 Remember
Screenshot the message, then show a trusted adult.
🤝

Don't Join In

If a group chat starts making fun of someone, staying quiet and joining in still hurts that person. Leaving the chat and telling an adult helps stop it.

⚠️ Watch Out
"Everyone else is doing it" is never a good reason to join in.
💛

Bonus: Be an Upstander, Not a Bystander

A bystander watches bullying happen and does nothing. An upstander speaks up, supports the person being bullied, or gets an adult involved. Being an upstander takes courage — but it makes a huge difference.

🏆 Pro move
If you see it happening to someone else, a quick private message like "that wasn't cool, are you okay?" can mean a lot.
