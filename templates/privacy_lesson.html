👁️ Privacy

Guard Your Personal Info!

Learn how to protect your personal information and stay safe online.

📍

Keep Personal Details Private

Information like your school, home address, phone number, and town can help strangers figure out where you are in real life.

Even if someone seems friendly online, you only know what they've chosen to tell you — which might not be true at all.

💡 Remember
Never share your address, school, phone number, or daily routine with people you meet online.
📸

Photos Can Reveal More Than You Think

Photos taken on phones and tablets can sometimes include hidden location information saved inside the file, called GPS data.

If that photo is shared online, it may reveal exactly where it was taken.

💡 Remember
Ask a parent or trusted adult before posting photos online, and keep location services turned off when sharing pictures.
📋

Be Careful with Online Forms

Fake competitions and giveaways often ask for your full name, address, phone number, or email to collect personal information.

A prize isn't worth giving away your privacy — real prizes don't need your home address to enter.

⚠️ Watch Out
Never fill out a form asking for personal information without asking a trusted adult first.
🔎

Bonus: Check Your Privacy Settings

Most apps and games let you choose who can see your profile, message you, or find you by username. Set profiles to "private" or "friends only" whenever you can.

🏆 Pro move
Ask a trusted adult to help you check the privacy settings on your favorite apps once a month.
