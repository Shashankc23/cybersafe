🔑 Two-Factor Authentication

A Second Lock on the Door!

Learn about the single best upgrade you can make to any account.

🔐

What Is 2FA?

Two-factor authentication (2FA) means logging in needs two things: your password, and a second code — usually sent to your phone or made by an app.

Even if a hacker steals your password, they still can't get in without that second code.

💡 Remember
Turn on 2FA anywhere it's offered, especially on your email and gaming accounts.
📱

Apps Are Better Than Texts

2FA codes can come by text message or through an authenticator app on your phone. Authenticator apps are generally the safer option, since text messages can sometimes be intercepted.

💡 Remember
If an app gives you the choice, pick an authenticator app over text messages when you can.
🚫

Never Share a 2FA Code

A 2FA code that pops up unexpectedly usually means someone else already has your password and is trying to log in right now.

⚠️ Watch Out
Never share a 2FA code with anyone, ever — not "support," not a friend, nobody. If one shows up that you didn't ask for, change your password immediately.
🗝️

Bonus: Protect Your Email First

Your email is often the master key to your other accounts — most sites let you reset a password just by emailing you a link. That makes your email the most important account to protect with 2FA.

🏆 Pro move
If you can only turn on 2FA for one account, make it your email.
