🪝 Phishing

Don't Get Hooked!

Learn the tricks scammers use before testing your skills.

🎭

Trick 1: Pretending to Be Someone Else

Phishing is when a scammer pretends to be a real company or person — like Roblox, YouTube, or even your school — to trick you into giving away information.

They copy logos, colors, and names so it looks real. But it's fake — it's just a costume. A scammer wearing a "Roblox" costume is still a scammer underneath.

💡 Remember
Always ask yourself: "Did I expect this message?" If you weren't expecting it, slow down before clicking anything.
🔗

Trick 2: Fake Website Addresses

Scammers use website addresses that look almost right, but have a sneaky difference hidden in the letters or numbers.

  • Real: roblox.com  |  Fake: r0blox.com (zero instead of the letter o!)
  • Real: minecraft.net  |  Fake: minecraft-helpdesk.net
  • Real: youtube.com  |  Fake: youtube-rewards.com
💡 Remember
Always check the address bar before clicking a link or typing a password. One extra word or a swapped letter means it's fake.
⏰

Trick 3: Fake Urgency

Scammers try to rush you so you don't have time to think it through. Watch out for messages like:

  • "Your account will be DELETED in 1 hour!"
  • "Claim your prize RIGHT NOW before it expires!"
  • "Act immediately or lose access forever!"
⚠️ Watch Out
If a message makes you panic or rush, slow down and tell a trusted adult. Real companies almost never give you a countdown timer.
🧠

Bonus: The 3-Second Check

Before you click, react, or reply to any message, do this 3-second check:

  • 1. Did I expect this?
  • 2. Does the address match the real company?
  • 3. Is it rushing me to act fast?
🏆 You're ready!
If you can answer those three questions, you're already better at spotting phishing than most adults.
