🌐 Safe Browsing

Stay Sharp Online!

Learn the habits that help you browse the internet safely.

🔍

Always Check the Address Bar

Scammers build websites that look almost identical to real ones, using the same logos, colors, and layouts.

The address bar is often the easiest way to spot the fake — copy the look, but they can't copy the real web address.

💡 Remember
Real: roblox.com
Fake: roblox-play-now.info

Always make sure the address matches the company you're trying to visit.
🔒

Look for the Little Padlock

Safe websites usually begin with https:// and show a small padlock icon next to the address.

Think of the padlock like a locked diary — it means no one else can peek at what you type while it travels to the website.

💡 Remember
Look for the padlock 🔒 before typing in passwords or personal information. No padlock? Be extra careful.
🚨

Watch Out for Downloads and Fake Pop-ups

Some websites try to scare or trick you into downloading harmful software.

  • A file starts downloading without you clicking anything.
  • A pop-up says "Virus Detected! Call this number now!"
⚠️ Watch Out
Close the tab, don't open unexpected downloads, and tell a trusted adult if something seems suspicious.
🧩

Bonus: Ads That Look Like Buttons

On some free game or movie sites, ads are made to look exactly like a big green "Download" or "Play" button — but clicking it takes you to a totally different, sketchy site.

🏆 Pro move
Look for the tiny word "Ad" near a button before clicking it. When in doubt, don't click — find the real button on the actual page.
