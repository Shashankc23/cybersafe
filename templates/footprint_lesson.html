👣 Digital Footprint

Think Before You Post!

Learn why what you post online can stick around way longer than you'd expect.

♾️

The Internet Doesn't Forget

Every photo, comment, and video you post leaves a trail called your digital footprint. Even if you delete something, people may have already saved, shared, or screenshotted it.

💡 Remember
Before you post, ask: "Would I be okay with anyone seeing this — forever?"
🙋

Ask Before You Post About Others

Posting a funny video of a friend might seem harmless, but it's their moment too. Always ask first — they might not want it online.

💡 Remember
If it includes someone else, get their okay before you hit post.
👀

Check the Background

Photos can accidentally show your school, street sign, or house number in the background. A trusted adult can help you double-check before posting.

⚠️ Watch Out
Scan the whole photo — not just the middle — before you share it.
🎓

Bonus: Future You Is Watching

Colleges, employers, and even coaches sometimes look up people online. What you post as a kid can still show up years later. It doesn't mean don't have fun online — just keep it something you'd be proud of later.

🏆 Pro move
Before posting, imagine a teacher or grandparent seeing it. Still okay? Post it.
