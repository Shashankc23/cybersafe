🔐 Passwords

Your Secret Key!

Learn the habits that make passwords strong and keep your accounts safe.

💀

Common Passwords Get Cracked in Seconds

Hackers use computer programs that try the world's most common passwords first — thousands of guesses per second. Passwords like password123 and 123456 get cracked almost instantly.

If millions of people use the exact same password, it isn't really a secret anymore.

💡 Remember
Never use simple passwords like password123, 123456, or easy number patterns like 111111.
🎮

Personal Details Are Easy to Guess

Your favorite game, pet's name, birthday, or school may seem like a good password — but anyone who knows you a little can guess them.

Even a password like minecraft2014 gives away a hint about you (your favorite game plus maybe your birth year).

💡 Remember
Avoid using your name, birthday, favorite game, pet's name, or anything someone could learn about you by looking at your profile.
💪

Strong Passwords Use Four Ingredients

Great passwords combine different types of characters to make them much harder for a computer to guess.

  • Uppercase letters (A–Z)
  • Lowercase letters (a–z)
  • Numbers (0–9)
  • Symbols (! @ # $ %)
💡 Remember
A password like MyD0g$N@medMax! is much stronger than max123 because it mixes letters, numbers, and symbols while still being easy for you to remember.
🔁

Bonus: Never Reuse Passwords

If you use the same password for your game account and your email, one leak means a hacker can get into everything. That's why big companies sometimes get "hacked" — a password from one leaked site gets tried everywhere else.

🏆 Pro move
Use a different password for your most important accounts — like email — than for random game sites.
