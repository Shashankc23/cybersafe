🎮 Gaming Safety

Play Smart, Stay Safe!

Learn how to spot the scams and tricks that target gamers.

🔑

Never Trade Your Login

No trade, rare skin, or "verification" is ever worth your account password. Scammers love offering amazing deals that require your login to "prove" something.

💡 Remember
Nobody legitimate — not a trader, not "support," not even a friend — ever needs your account password.
💸

Skip the Cheap Currency Sites

Third-party websites selling in-game currency for way less than the official store are almost always scams. They either steal your payment info or your account.

⚠️ Watch Out
Always buy in-game currency or items through the official store — even if it costs a little more.
🎁

"Special Tools" Are Usually Malware

Strangers offering aim-assist tools, free skins generators, or "boosting" software are one of the most common ways malware ends up on your device.

💡 Remember
Only download mods or tools from the game's official site or well-known, trusted sources.
🔇

Bonus: Mute and Report, Don't Engage

If someone in a game is threatening you, being inappropriate, or pressuring you for anything, you don't have to respond. Muting and reporting is faster, safer, and actually gets them dealt with.

🏆 Pro move
Arguing back rarely works. Mute, report, and if it feels serious, tell a trusted adult too.
